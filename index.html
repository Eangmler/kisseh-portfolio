DIRECTOR'S VISION

Stories first. Always rooted in human truth.

I've always been curious about why people do what they do. That curiosity has taken me through theatre, filmmaking, photography, music, creative strategy, and the systems that hold organisations together.

"Sometimes that curiosity becomes a film. Sometimes it's a campaign, a song, or just a conversation that changes how someone sees themselves."

I studied Theatre Arts at the University of Ghana, where performance became my first real language for communication. That foundation — understanding how to hold an audience, how to move a story forward, how to make people feel something — runs through everything else I do.

Since then I've worked as a creative director for emerging brands, produced a healthcare podcast from concept to launch, shot and edited music videos, and contributed original songs to an internationally staged theatrical production in the Netherlands.

I'm also the creator of Think Out Loud with Kisseh — a developing conversation series built around one question: What makes people think the way they do?

Whether I'm behind a camera, in an editing suite, on stage, or deep inside a creative brief — the work is always about connecting ideas to the people they were made for.
