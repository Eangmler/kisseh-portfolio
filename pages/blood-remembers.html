The Production

A story that demanded to be told. And believed.

The Blood Remembers is a stage play in which Kisseh performed in a lead role. What drew him to it was simpler and more personal than craft: he could recognise himself inside the story.

That kind of connection to material changes how you perform. It shifts the work from technical execution into something that has real weight — because you're not just playing a character, you're bringing part of yourself to it.

"I believed in the story because I could see a part of myself in it."
