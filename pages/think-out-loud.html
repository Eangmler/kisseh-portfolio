A conversation and documentary series exploring the hidden thinking behind people's decisions, careers, identities, and lives. Not just what people do — but why they do it.

The Idea

Reverse-engineering how people think

Most interviews ask what. Think Out Loud asks why.

The series is built around one persistent question: what is the hidden thinking behind the decisions people make? Not the polished version of events someone gives at a press conference — but the actual reasoning, the fears, the values, the moments that changed things. The thinking underneath the story.

Each conversation is designed to go somewhere unexpected. Not through confrontation, but through genuine curiosity — the kind that comes from someone who has already spent time understanding the other person's world.

"Understanding the hidden thinking behind people's decisions."

Format

Conversations with cinematic DNA

Think Out Loud is not a conventional podcast. It combines personal conversation, documentary storytelling, psychological and philosophical questioning, and cinematic visual storytelling. The intention is for each episode to feel like a film that also happens to be a conversation.

The show draws on existing relationships — guests are people Kisseh knows, has learned from, or has watched navigate their lives in ways worth examining. That intimacy changes the quality of the conversation.
