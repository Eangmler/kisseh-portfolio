The Story

Human ingenuity rooted in local soil

Filmed on location in the Eastern Region of Ghana, A Story of Resilience follows an inspiring woman who turned a staple food — gari — into an innovative source of comprehensive nutrition. Faced with everyday community dietary challenges, she developed a distinctive technique of pairing gari with wholesome ingredients to create an accessible, nourishing mix.

The documentary captures the quiet dignity of her labour, the chemistry of her craft, and the ripple effects on local families who depend on her ingenuity.

Direction & Craft

Directing, Editing & Coloring

As Director, Editor, and Colorist, my aim was to avoid poverty clichés and instead capture the warmth, texture, and natural intelligence of rural entrepreneurship. The color palette preserves the rich earth tones of the Eastern Region — deep red soil, golden gari grains, and vivid natural daylight.

Pacing in the editing suite was designed to let moments breathe, allowing the subject's own cadence and hands-on process to lead the emotional rhythm of the film.
