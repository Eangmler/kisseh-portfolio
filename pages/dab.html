The Brief

Healthcare conversations that people actually want to hear.

FLOKEFAMA Company Limited — a healthcare technology, diagnostics, and biomedical engineering firm — wanted to create a media platform that made healthcare conversations more accessible, engaging, and human. Not clinical briefings. Not dry informational content. Real conversations, with real people, about health and everything connected to it.

That became DAB — Diagnostics & Beyond. And I built it from the name up.
